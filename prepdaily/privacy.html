PrepDaily — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

PrepDaily is a daily quiz app for competitive exam preparation. This policy explains what the app does and does not collect. It is short because the app does very little with data.

We do not ask you to create an account

PrepDaily has no sign-up, no login and no user profile. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number or date of birth, and we have no way to identify you as an individual.

Your practice history stays on your device

Your answers, streak, scores and progress are stored only in your phone's local storage. They are never uploaded to us or to anyone else. We operate no server that receives your practice data, and we cannot see it. The usage statistics described below never include it.

Uninstalling the app, or using "Reset all progress" inside it, permanently deletes this data. Because it is never transmitted, we hold no copy to delete on your behalf.

Usage statistics and crash reports

The app uses Google Firebase to send anonymous usage statistics: which features are used, for example that a daily quiz or a mock paper was started or finished, that the Pro screen was opened, or that a result was shared, together with the question language you chose. When you buy Pro or start the Pro Pass, Firebase also records the purchase itself: which plan, and its price. These events never contain your payment details, and never contain your answers, scores, streak or anything you type, and never your name, email address or phone number. With them, Firebase records an identifier for this installation of the app, your device's advertising identifier, approximate location derived from your IP address, the app version and general device information such as model and operating system version.

If the app crashes, Firebase Crashlytics sends a crash report: the technical details of the error, the app version, and the device model and operating system version.

We use this to see how many people use the app and which features help them, to measure whether our advertising reaches people who actually use the app, and to find and fix crashes. Google processes it on our behalf under the Firebase terms; see firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, usage statistics are not collected.

Advertising

The free version of PrepDaily displays banner advertisements supplied by Google AdMob. It also offers optional rewarded video advertisements, up to eight a day, each shown only when you choose to watch it: to unlock ten more questions, an extra practice session or an SSC or bank mock paper, to open the full analysis or the calm soundscapes for the rest of the day, or to keep a streak you missed a day of. At most twice a day, at least five minutes apart, and never in your first two days with the app or on a day you have watched a rewarded video, it shows a full-screen advertisement as you leave the result of a quiz, practice session or mock paper you have finished; never while you are answering. To serve any of these, AdMob may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising identifier and app set ID;
  • approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • how you interact with the app and its ads, such as app launches, taps and video views;
  • diagnostic information, such as app launch time and how the ads performed;
  • general device information, such as model and operating system version.

Google uses this for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. This collection is performed by Google, not by us, and is governed by Google's own privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your device's settings, under Privacy or Ads.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before any ad is requested, and More → Ad privacy choices lets you change your answer at any time.

While you have Pro, bought once or as the Pro Pass, advertisements are not requested or displayed.

Purchases

In-app purchases, including the Pro Pass subscription, are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your payment card details. Google tells the app only whether a purchase was successful or a subscription is active, and that result is recorded locally on your device.

The Pro Pass renews automatically, each year or each month, until you cancel it in the Google Play Store under Payments & subscriptions. Where a free trial is offered, it becomes the paid plan when it ends unless you cancel before then.

Notifications

If you switch on the daily reminder, it is scheduled locally on your device. The app uses no push notification service, so no device token is created or transmitted, and we cannot send you a message.

Children

PrepDaily is intended for adults and older students preparing for competitive examinations. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from them.

Rating the app

The app can ask, at most once and only after you have used it for a while, whether you would like to rate it. The prompt is Google Play's own in-app review dialog: the app asks Play to show it and is told nothing about what you do next, including whether you rated it or what you said.

Data we share

We do not sell your data or share it with anyone else. The third-party software development kits in the app are Google AdMob, Google Firebase (Analytics and Crashlytics), Google Play Billing and Google Play's in-app review dialog, all described above.
