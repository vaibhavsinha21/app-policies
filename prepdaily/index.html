PrepDaily — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 August 2026

PrepDaily is a daily quiz app for competitive exam preparation. This policy explains what the app does and does not collect. It is short because the app does very little with data.

We do not ask you to create an account

PrepDaily has no sign-up, no login and no user profile. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number or date of birth, and we have no way to identify you as an individual.

Your practice history stays on your device

Your answers, streak, scores and progress are stored only in your phone's local storage. They are never uploaded to us or to anyone else. We operate no server that receives your practice data, and we cannot see it.

Uninstalling the app, or using "Reset all progress" inside it, permanently deletes this data. Because it is never transmitted, we hold no copy to delete on your behalf.

Advertising

The free version of PrepDaily displays banner advertisements supplied by Google AdMob. To serve these, AdMob may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising identifier;
  • approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • general device information, such as model and operating system version.

This collection is performed by Google, not by us, and is governed by Google's own privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your device's settings, under Privacy or Ads.

Purchasing the ad-free upgrade stops advertisements from being requested or displayed.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your payment card details. Google tells the app only whether a purchase was successful, and that result is recorded locally on your device.

Notifications

If you switch on the daily reminder, it is scheduled locally on your device. The app uses no push notification service, so no device token is created or transmitted, and we cannot send you a message.

Children

PrepDaily is intended for adults and older students preparing for competitive examinations. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from them.

Data we share

None. We have no analytics, no crash reporting service, no third-party software development kits other than Google AdMob and Google Play Billing described above, and no data of yours to share.
