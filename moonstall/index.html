Moonstall

A cozy night market.

Start with one scallion-pancake griddle by a temple at dusk, and build a night market along its lane: stalls, tables, a games stall, a musician. A crowd with minds of its own comes to eat, queue, sit and play, and the market grows through four ages of Taiwan's night-market history. Hire helpers and it keeps cooking while you are away. Then defend it: the Nian beast raids it, rival chefs from six cities challenge it on the lei tai, the dragon dances through it, and a phoenix comes to it once it is at peace. And play its story, The Tale of the Moonlight Market: 42 levels across six cities, from temple processions to the brood's bouncing knots.

Made with care

  • Every stall, visitor, lantern and sound is drawn and made in code: the sizzle, the crowd, the temple drum, and a moon lute that plays as you cook, never the same twice.
  • An original score, the Moonlight Market Suite: six themes composed for the game, over a guqin and strings, and varied every time they come back.
  • Every visitor wants something, and says so: hungry, thirsty, a sweet tooth, tired feet, or fun. Eighteen regulars across the island come once the market has what they love.
  • Ten real dishes, from scallion pancakes and pepper buns to gua bao and mango shaved ice.
  • Calm by design: no timers to beat, no energy, and nothing for sale that skips the wait.
  • Switches for music, sounds, vibration and reduce motion. Every screen holds at large text sizes, and every plot and button speaks to a screen reader.
  • No banners and no ads that interrupt. Watch a short video only if you want a festival, to double a return, or to call the dragon dancers.
